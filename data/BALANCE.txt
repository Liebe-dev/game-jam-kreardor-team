Balance ownership

Unit definitions in data/units/*.tres hold base statistics. Wave rosters and
reinforcement values remain in data/waves/*.tres. logicgame.gd applies per-wave
enemy overrides to individual copies before they enter the scene; it does not
mutate the shared definitions. Retry and continue use the same spawn path.
Original enemy rosters and positions are unchanged.

Spell values, paper costs and fixed base ink costs are in data/spells/*.tres.
The spell catalog owns the 0.65 multi-target factor. The resolver validates
first and charges on a successful cast: second target and each direction add
20 ink. Text edits and aliases are free. Invalid or unaffordable casts are
not charged. Spells no longer create ink or paper. Clearing waves 1 through 4
awards 3 paper, 250 ink and heals the King by 20 before checkpointing.

These are initial balance values, to be validated with human playtesting.
