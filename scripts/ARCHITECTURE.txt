Refactor interfaces

logicgame.gd composes the board and campaign. Existing scene signal handlers and
legacy helper methods remain as compatibility entry points for existing scenes
and regression tests; their implementations delegate to the owning module.

gameplay/turn_resolver.gd
  resolve_turn() -> Dictionary {accepted, turn, wave, finished}
  Owns reentry lock and movement/combat/terrain/buff-expiry ordering.
  Node lifetime follows the board, including pending asynchronous turn resolution.

gameplay/combat_resolver.gd
  Owns unit AI, attack allowance, damage, grabs, knockback and ammunition.
  Receives board context instead of looking up /root/board.
  attack_started and damage_resolved expose gameplay results.
  attack_animation_requested and projectile_requested are presentation requests.

presentation/battle_presentation.gd
  Subscribes to presentation requests. It does not decide attack counts or damage.
  Disconnecting presentation listeners leaves turn and damage results unchanged.

data/unit_definition.gd, wave_definition.gd, wave_spawn.gd
  Immutable shared definitions. Mutable runtime state remains on each Piece/board.

gameplay/spell_resolver.gd
  request_cast(elements, coordinates, directions) -> Dictionary {ok, reason/metadata}
  Validates the command, pays paper and commits the once-per-turn allowance.
  pay_for_edit(previous, proposed) owns character ink payment.
  Legacy board effect methods delegate here for trusted internal/test use.

gameplay/tile_effect_system.gd
  Owns active_effects, periodic effects, propagation, expiry and wall creation.
  Reads the same SpellCatalog as SpellResolver. BattlePresentation creates visuals.

data/spell_definition.gd and spell_catalog.gd
  Fourteen immutable spell definitions provide strength, lifetime, rewards, cost
  and type-specific settings. The spellbook renders describe() from these objects.

board.gd connects cast_requested to the resolver and spell_resolved back to UI.
magic_drawing_system.gd displays drafts/results and requests input payment; it does
not debit resources or dispatch gameplay by groups. Global.resources_changed is an
event; Global no longer calls into a magic UI node to refresh resource displays.
